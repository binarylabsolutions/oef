# Creator admissions

*Last updated: 30 Sep 2026 · Not final*

We can't give full support to every creator who applies. Instead of one hard rule (like "50k followers"), we use **three levels of support** and **three ways in**, with the number of places set by how many volunteer hours we actually have.

## Why not just a follower threshold

- **It shuts out the people we most want:** good teachers who are new, from smaller cities, women, or making content in regional languages.
- **Followers can be bought**, and they measure popularity, not teaching quality or accuracy.
- **A big account is not the same as a good fit:** some large accounts would break the charter on day one.

Traction still counts; it just isn't the only way in.

## Three levels of support

| Level | Who | What they get | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Open** | Any education creator who accepts the charter | Free tools and templates, the playbook, the community Discord, workshops, office hours | None |
| **Supported** | Admitted creators (below) | Everything in Open, plus a limited monthly allowance of volunteer hours (e.g. 10 h) for reviews, covers, subtitles | Set by spare volunteer capacity |
| **Core** | Supported creators who have done well for 3+ months | Full studio backend: a matched team, editing, fact-checking, series planning, collabs, paid experts when funded | A few per stage (see roadmap) |

Everyone can start at **Open** right away, so nobody is turned away from the community; the limits apply only to volunteer hours.

## Three ways into Supported

| Route | Who it's for | What's needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Traction** | Creators who already have an audience | At least 12 education videos in the last 3 months, and a real, engaged audience: e.g. 10k+ followers **and** an engagement rate at or above 3%, or a median of 10k+ views per video |
| **Vouch** | New or small creators with real promise | A vouch from a Core creator or a member of the subject-expert panel, explaining in writing why this creator is worth backing |
| **Open application** | Anyone else, especially teachers new to video | 3 sample videos (can be rough) reviewed by the admissions panel |

All three routes then go through **the same review**. A vouch or big numbers get you a faster review, not an automatic place.

## The review

**Admissions panel (3 people):** one subject expert in the creator's field, one Core creator, one core-team member.

**Scoring (out of 100)**

| Criterion | Points | What the panel looks at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | 30 | The panel fact-checks 3 of the creator's videos. **Must score at least 20**, or the application stops here |
| Teaching quality | 25 | Is one idea explained clearly? Would a learner come away understanding something? |
| Audience response | 15 | Engagement rate, comments that are real questions, saves and shares (not raw followers) |
| Consistency | 10 | Regular posting over the last 3 months |
| Fit with our priorities | 10 | In scope, fits the [content strategy](11-content-strategy.md); already makes Urdu content or commits to an Urdu version of every piece ([language policy](11-content-strategy.md#language-policy-urdu-first)); bonus for underserved subjects and regions |
| Collaboration | 10 | A short call: willing to follow the charter, credit, correct mistakes, give feedback |

- **60 or more** → admitted to Supported (when a place is free).
- **45–59** → stays at Open with written feedback; can reapply after 3 months.
- **Under 45 or accuracy under 20** → stays at Open with feedback; can reapply after 6 months.

Decisions are by majority vote of the panel, and the reasons are written down and shared with the creator.

## Vouching rules

- Each Core creator or expert can vouch for **2 creators a year**.
- The voucher is accountable: if a vouched creator seriously breaks the charter in their first 6 months, the voucher's next vouch is paused for a year.
- Vouchers can't vouch for family members or business partners.

## Places and waiting list

- Each quarter, the ops lead works out spare volunteer hours and opens that many Supported places (e.g. 150 spare hours a month ÷ 10 hours per creator = 15 places).
- Applications are reviewed in **quarterly cohorts**, not one by one, so the panel isn't swamped.
- **Reserved places:** at least a third of each cohort goes to women creators, creators outside Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad, or creators working in regional languages, as long as they pass the review.
- Everyone else who passes goes on a waiting list, ordered by score.

## Moving up, staying, and leaving

| From → to | Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| Supported → Core | 3+ months at Supported; used volunteer hours well (on-time feedback, credits every time); a correction handled properly if one came up; a Core place is free |
| Probation | First 3 months at any new level; a check-in each month |
| Back to Open | Charter broken (no credits, refusing a correction, out-of-scope content), or no posting for 2 months. The creator is told why and can reapply later |

## Starting point

- **Rohail Sarfraz is the pilot creator** and starts at Core directly, because the pilot is how we build and test this whole system.
- Until the subject-expert panel exists (Stage 2), the core team plus one outside subject expert act as the admissions panel.
- The thresholds above (10k followers, 3% engagement, 10 hours a month) are starting values, reviewed after the first two cohorts.
