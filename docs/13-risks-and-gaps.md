# Risks and gaps

*Last updated: 30 Sep 2026*

What we should worry about, and what the system still needs. Ordered by how badly each could hurt us if ignored.

## The biggest risk: doing everything

The vision covers creators, volunteers, paid experts, teacher tools, student tools, TV shows, documentaries and out-of-school children. Each is worth doing; doing them all at once guarantees none is done well. **Rule:** one new major track per year, only after the previous one passes its gate ([07-roadmap.md](07-roadmap.md), [12-long-term-plan.md](12-long-term-plan.md)).

## Risks

| # | Risk | Why it matters | What we add to the system |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Child safety** | Much of our audience, and some contributors, will be under 18 | A safeguarding policy: no private messages between adults and minors, guardian consent, two adults on any shoot with children, a named safeguarding lead, a way to report concerns |
| 2 | **A science error or misinformation** | One viral mistake damages trust in every creator | A subject-expert review panel (volunteer teachers, professors); sources linked for every video; the corrections policy with correction videos and a public corrections log ([charter section 4](03-charter.md#4-corrections-owning-our-mistakes)) |
| 3 | **Legal: religion, politics, cybercrime law** | Content can be reported under strict laws, with severe consequences | Clear content scope ([11-content-strategy.md](11-content-strategy.md)); sensitive-topic review before publishing; legal adviser on call; no party politics, no sectarian content |
| 4 | **Founder as single point of failure** | Everything depends on one person's time and judgement | A board of 3–5 trustees by the time we register; written playbooks; a deputy for every lead |
| 5 | **Platform dependence** | YouTube was blocked in Pakistan from 2012 to 2016; algorithms and bans change overnight | Our own content library and website; WhatsApp channels; downloadable, offline copies of everything |
| 6 | **Money: donor dependence and mission drift** | A big donor can pull out or push an agenda | Public ledger; no single donor over 30% of the budget; refuse money with strings attached ([08-public-ledger.md](08-public-ledger.md)) |
| 7 | **Copyright** | Music, film clips and textbook images get videos taken down | A shared library of licensed and Creative Commons music, footage and icons; a copyright check in every edit checklist |
| 8 | **Creator scandal** | A creator's personal controversy splashes onto the studio | Code of conduct for creators; the right to pause studio support; no creator is bigger than the charter |
| 9 | **Quality drift across many creators** | "Studio" becomes a label with no meaning | A "studio-reviewed" standard with checklists; only reviewed content carries the studio name |
| 10 | **Volunteer burnout and churn** | The engine stalls | Small tasks, easy pause, monthly pledge renewal, recognition ([04-studio-operations.md](04-studio-operations.md)) |
| 11 | **Measuring views instead of learning** | We optimise for the algorithm, not for education | An impact framework (below) |
| 12 | **Excluding people** | Content only reaches urban, male, English- or Urdu-literate viewers | Accessibility and inclusion (below) |
| 13 | **Data and account security** | Leaked contributor data or hacked creator accounts | Minimal data, two-factor login everywhere, no shared passwords, privacy policy |
| 14 | **Government and political capture** | Partnerships can bring pressure on content | Partner on distribution, never on editorial control; this is written into every partnership |

## Gaps: things the system doesn't have yet

| Gap | What to add | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Editorial standards | A written style and accuracy guide; subject-expert review panel; corrections log | Stage 2 |
| Safeguarding | Policy, lead, reporting route, training for anyone who works with minors | Before any content with children |
| Shared asset library | Licensed music, footage, icons, templates, series branding | Stage 2 |
| Content library | Every video tagged by subject, grade, board, language and ladder step, openly searchable | Stage 3 |
| Localisation pipeline | Subtitles and dubs into Urdu, English, Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, Balochi, Saraiki | Stage 3 |
| Creator incubator | Train new teacher-creators, with priority for women and teachers from smaller cities and regions | Stage 3 |
| Impact measurement | Learning checks, not just views | Stage 2 pilot |
| Governance | Board of trustees, conflict-of-interest policy, annual public report | At registration |
| Crisis-response content | Fast, accurate public-service explainers during floods, heatwaves, disease outbreaks | Stage 3 |
| Offline distribution kit | Downloadable packs for schools and NGOs without reliable internet | Year 2–3 |

## Impact framework

We track four levels. Views alone never count as success.

| Level | Question | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reach | Did people see it? | Views, unique viewers, share of women and of non-urban viewers |
| Engagement | Did they care? | Watch-through, comments that are questions, saves, shares |
| Learning | Did they understand? | Quiz scores before and after, in partner schools and online |
| Action | Did they do something? | Experiments tried, projects shared, teachers using our material in class |

## Accessibility and inclusion

- Subtitles on every video, in at least Urdu and English.
- Voice-first formats for viewers who can't read.
- Pakistan Sign Language versions of flagship content (partner with deaf-community organisations).
- Women creators and women contributors actively recruited; a harassment policy that protects them.
- Content made in, and about, every province, not just Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad.
- Low-data versions: audio-only and small-file downloads.
