# Research: the education YouTube landscape

*Compiled 30 Sep 2026. Subscriber counts are from Wikipedia and aggregators at the dates shown; re-check before quoting publicly.*

## Key takeaways for us

1. **Kids' education and exam prep are the largest education niches by views; science edutainment is the largest by global brand.** Our biggest open lanes in Pakistan: Urdu kids' curiosity content and Urdu science edutainment.
2. **The strongest education brands are teams, not individuals, and are often non-profit or grant-funded.** Complexly and Khan Academy are non-profits; Kurzgesagt is ~70 people funded by merch, Patreon, ads and grants (including the Gates Foundation).
3. **Physics Wallah proves free YouTube teaching builds huge trust in South Asia** (it went from a YouTube channel to a stock-exchange listing). We take the trust lesson, not the paid-course model.
4. **Short-form helps discovery but the evidence on driving long-form is mixed.** One study found education channels relatively unaffected by adding Shorts; design matters more than length.
5. **Faceless is not the problem, low quality is.** Kurzgesagt is faceless; YouTube now demonetises mass-produced "inauthentic" AI content, and AI pseudo-science has been pushed to children.

## Complexly: the model we are borrowing from

- Became a **non-profit in February 2026**; the founders gave up their equity ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/02/04/hank-john-green-vlogbrothers-complexly-nonprofit/)).
- 10,000+ videos; SciShow, Crash Course, PBS Eons ([complexly.org](https://www.complexly.org/)); 70+ staff ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complexly)).
- Crash Course was funded over time by a YouTube channel fund, fan funding (Subbable, later Patreon), PBS Digital Studios, YouTube Learning, and Arizona State University ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crash_Course_(YouTube))). With ASU it offers YouTube-first courses worth college credit ([ASU](https://news.asu.edu/20230124-solutions-partnership-asu-youtube-crash-course-expands-offering-college-credit)).
- **Lesson for us:** funding came from a mix of platform funds, fans, public media and universities, never one source.

## Short-form and learning

- Shorts average 200B daily views ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-reports-200-billion-daily-views-for-shorts-format/549315/)). YouTube recommends linking each Short to a related long video, a call to action in the last 5 seconds, and paying off the promise in the first 5–10 seconds of the long video ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-related-videos-traffic-guide/)).
- Evidence on Shorts driving long-form is mixed: a 250-channel study found long-form views fell after adding Shorts, but education channels were relatively unaffected ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18208v2)); an 18,000-channel analysis found mixed channels with 25–40% Shorts did best, correlation only ([AIR](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels)).
- A meta-analysis of 71 studies (98,299 people) links heavy short-video use to poorer attention and inhibitory control, mostly correlational ([PsyPost summary](https://www.psypost.org/large-meta-analysis-links-tiktok-and-instagram-reels-to-poorer-cognitive-and-mental-health/)). A small experiment found reels made from lecture content beat full lectures on quiz scores ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05962)). **Design matters more than length.**

## Faceless and AI content

- Since 15 Jul 2025 YouTube calls mass-produced, templated AI content "inauthentic" and doesn't monetise it ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)); AI tools themselves remain allowed ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/)).
- Realistic synthetic content (cloned voices, real-looking fake events) must be labelled; animation and AI-written scripts need no label ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content/)).
- Low-quality AI videos are widespread, including in Pakistan's trending lists ([Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/)); AI pseudo-science channels were found targeting children ([report](https://dailyai.com/2023/09/youtube-channels-are-targeting-children-with-ai-generated-disinformation/)).
