# Proposal: Open Education Studio × Lahore Garrison University

*Prepared 30 Sep 2026 · For discussion with the university*

## In short

We invite Lahore Garrison University to be a founding university partner of the Open Education Studio, a non-profit that gives Pakistan's education creators a free team of volunteers, tools and systems, and keeps everything it helps make free for everyone.

We propose a **one-semester pilot**: your students join as volunteer "Studio Fellows" and do real work on real education videos, and in return we bring workshops, creator seminars and on-campus events, and credit every student publicly for their work. No fees either way.

## Why this is good for the university and its students

- **Real experience with a public record:** students work on published education content and software, and every contribution is credited by name, a portfolio they can show employers.
- **Skills that matter now:** workshops in software engineering, AI tools, open source, video, design, science communication and fact-checking.
- **Final-year projects with real users:** the studio's platform is open source and needs real systems built.
- **Visibility:** well-known education creators on campus; the university credited as a founding partner on the work its students help make.
- **Social impact:** 25.1 million children in Pakistan are out of school and 80% of children can't read with understanding by the end of primary school ([why we exist](../../02-mission.md)). Students help build free education for everyone.

## What we propose for the pilot semester

| The studio will | The university is asked to |
| --- | --- |
| Run **4 workshops** during the semester (2 technical by our software-engineer founder, 2 creative or science-communication) | Help form a **cohort of 20–30 student volunteers**, through a society, club or department |
| Host **1 creator seminar** with an education creator (our pilot creator, science educator Rohail Sarfraz, 49k+ followers) | Name a **faculty coordinator** as our main contact |
| Hold **1 creator shoot day** on campus, e.g. "Street Questions" with students | Provide **rooms for workshops and events**, and a space for the shoot day |
| Place Fellows in **real work**: research, fact-checking, editing, design, and building our open-source platform | Where possible, give Fellows **co-curricular recognition** |
| Offer **final-year project topics** co-mentored with a faculty supervisor | Invite interested **faculty experts** to join our fact-check panel |
| Award **certificates** based on delivered work, and **reference letters** for consistent contributors | Host a **semester showcase** |
| Publish a **semester report**: students involved, work delivered, videos credited | Review the pilot with us at the end of the semester |

## How it works for a student

1. **Weeks 1–2:** orientation and kick-off workshops.
2. **Week 3:** a discovery round of three short try-out challenges to find each student's strengths.
3. **Weeks 4–12:** real work in one of four tracks (content, production, technology, operations), with a few hours a week pledged by each student.
4. **Week 13:** showcase; certificates awarded for delivered work.

Participation is always voluntary, and students can pause at any time.

## Our commitments

- **Free:** no fees for the university or students, now or later.
- **Credit:** every student named on the work they did; the university credited as a partner.
- **Education only:** no advertising, no selling, no politics.
- **Accuracy and honesty:** every video fact-checked; mistakes corrected in public; AI use always disclosed.
- **Consent and safety:** written consent from anyone filmed; students' personal data kept private.
- **No exclusivity, no binding:** a simple letter of intent for the pilot; a formal, non-binding MoU once the studio completes its non-profit registration.

3. Set dates for the kick-off workshop and the creator seminar.
4. Recruit the first cohort.
