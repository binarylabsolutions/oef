# Letter of intent: pilot partnership (draft)

*Draft for discussion · To be reviewed by both sides before signing · The formal MoU follows once the studio is a registered entity*

**Between:** Lahore Garrison University ("the University") and the Open Education Studio initiative ("the Studio"), represented by its founder.

**Period:** one academic semester, from ________ to ________.

## 1. Purpose

To run a one-semester pilot in which University students volunteer with the Studio as "Studio Fellows", and the Studio provides workshops, creator events and real project experience, in support of free education in Pakistan.

## 2. The University intends to

1. Help form a cohort of about 20–30 student volunteers.
2. Name a faculty coordinator as the main contact.
3. Provide rooms for workshops and events, and a space for one creator shoot day, subject to availability.
4. Consider co-curricular recognition for Fellows, under its own rules.
5. Invite interested faculty to act as subject experts for fact-checking.
6. Host an end-of-semester showcase.

## 3. The Studio intends to

1. Deliver 4 workshops during the semester.
2. Host 1 seminar with an education creator on campus.
3. Hold 1 creator shoot day on campus.
4. Give Fellows real tasks with guidance and review.
5. Offer final-year project topics, co-mentored with a faculty supervisor.
6. Award certificates for delivered work and reference letters for consistent contributors.
7. Share a written semester report with the University.

## 4. Principles

1. **No payment** is made by either party to the other, or to or by students, under this letter.
2. **Voluntary participation:** students join and leave freely; participation is never a condition of any grade.
3. **Credit:** students are credited by name (or chosen handle) on work they contribute to; the University is credited as a partner on work made with its students or on its campus.
4. **Editorial independence:** the University has no control over the content of the Studio's or its creators' videos; faculty may advise on factual accuracy.
5. **No advertising:** content made under this pilot does not promote the University beyond partner credit.
6. **Licensing:** work contributed by students is used under the Studio's open licences, as set out in its charter; students keep the right to show their work in their portfolios.
7. **Consent and privacy:** written consent is obtained from anyone filmed; students' personal data is used only to run the programme and is never published without consent.
8. **Conduct:** all participants follow the Studio's charter, including accuracy, corrections and AI disclosure.
9. **No exclusivity:** either party may work with other organisations.

## 5. Nature of this letter

This letter records shared intentions. It is **not legally binding** and creates no financial obligations. Either party may end the pilot at any time with written notice. At the end of the semester the parties will review the pilot and decide whether to sign a formal memorandum of understanding.
