### What works
- **Teacher POV skits** are the engine: 4 reels averaging about 216k views, roughly 10x everything else.
- **Wide reach and strong engagement:** teacher POV, Iron Man, HLLM, Rickshaw physics.
- **Loyal core:** personal and charity reels (Journey 6.7%, HOME 6.0%) get small reach but the highest engagement.
- **Sci-fi hooks drive comments:** Iron Man got 429 comments, the most in this period.

### What doesn't
- **History series** is below median on both reach and engagement: it needs a stronger hook, not more episodes in the same format.
- **Generic hashtags** (#viral #trending #fyp #aryankelvin), no niche or series tags.
- **Series are hard to follow:** no episode numbers on covers, no pinned entry point, no highlight.
- **Slips:** one reel carries a copied caption about a K-pop star; "Journey so far" was posted twice on 25 Aug.
- **No funnel:** nothing captures the 487k viewers (no community, list or link-in-bio offer).

## 3. The operating system

### Tools
| Tool | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| ClickUp | Idea bank, production pipeline, series tracker, calendar |
| Discord | Communication, approvals, contributor tasks |
| Google Drive | Raw footage, edits, covers; a folder per reel named with its ClickUp ID |
| Performance sheet | One row per reel: views at 24 h and 7 days, likes, comments, shares, saves, follows, format, series |
| Canva | One cover template per series, with episode number |

### Pipeline (ClickUp statuses)
`Idea → Approved → Scripted → Scheduled for shoot → Shot → In edit → Review → Ready (buffer) → Posted → Reviewed`

Each card holds: format, series + episode number, hook line, caption draft, hashtag set, cover text, target date, and the contributors to credit.

### Weekly rhythm
| When | What | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | 30-min call: last week's numbers, pick next week's 4 posts from the buffer | Studio + Rohail |
| Tuesday | Script 4–6 ideas | Studio drafts, Rohail edits |
| Wed or Sat | Batch shoot: 6–8 reels in one session | Rohail |
| Thu–Fri | Editing, covers, captions | Contributors |
| Daily | Post at the fixed slot; reply to first-hour comments | Studio |
| Month end | Review: what to scale, cut, test | Studio + Rohail |

### Buffer
- Keep **8 finished reels** ready at all times (2 weeks of posting).
- Build it with two batch-shoot days in the first 3 weeks.
- Keep 2 evergreen reels for emergencies (illness, exams, travel).

### Posting checklist
- Series cover with episode number.
- Caption: hook line, 1–2 lines of value, a question for comments, call to action, credit block.
- Hashtags: 3 niche (e.g. #physics #urduscience #pakistaniteachers) + 2 broad + 1 series tag.
- Duplicate check; no copied captions.
- Cross-post every reel to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook from month 1. In Pakistan, TikTok reaches 79.9m people and YouTube 54.3m, against 22.4m on Instagram ([research](../../research/pakistan-education-context.md)).
