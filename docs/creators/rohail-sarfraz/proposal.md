# Proposal for Rohail Sarfraz

*Prepared 30 Sep 2026 · To present in person*

## In short

We'd like you to be the first creator of the Open Education Studio: a non-profit that gives education creators a free team and a system. You keep making the content you're known for; we take care of planning, scripting help, fact-checking, editing, design, posting and analytics. It costs you nothing and you stay fully independent.

## What we see on your account

- **A real audience:** 49.3k followers and reels reaching 487k (physics teacher POV) and 288k (maths teacher POV) views in the last six weeks.
- **A format that works:** your "teacher sees daily life through their subject" skits average about 216k views, roughly 10x your other reels.
- **Strong engagement:** Rickshaw-driver physics, your Biology POV and your HLLM movement reel all get 5.6–6% likes and comments per view, above your median of 4.5%.
- **What's holding it back:** no content calendar, no backlog of ready videos, series that are hard to follow, generic hashtags, and a few slips (a copied caption, a duplicate post). None of this is about talent; it's about having a system and a team.

## What we offer (free, always)

| We do | So that you |
| --- | --- |
| Run a content calendar and keep 8 finished reels in reserve | Post 4 times a week, even in busy weeks |
| Turn your best formats into named series with covers and numbering | Build loyal viewers who follow every episode |
| Draft scripts in your style, and fact-check every script | Spend your time on ideas and on camera |
| Edit, add subtitles, design covers, write captions and hashtags | Get polished reels without the late nights |
| Weekly numbers and a monthly review | Know exactly what works and do more of it |
| Bring in collabs with other education creators | Grow faster together |

Details are in the [growth and systems plan](growth-plan.md).

## What we ask in return

1. **Credit the team.** Every reel made with studio help names the people who worked on it, in the caption.
2. **Keep it free.** Education content made with studio help stays free to watch and always available. Your own paid classes, tuition and workshops are entirely yours; we just don't use volunteer hours on paid material. (Full rules: [charter](../../03-charter.md).)
3. **Own mistakes in public.** If a reel turns out to be wrong, you make a short correction video that explains what was wrong, teaches the right answer, and thanks whoever caught it. Corrections build trust, and they show students how science actually works. ([Corrections policy](../../03-charter.md#4-corrections-owning-our-mistakes))
4. **Be open about AI.** Keep a short public note on how you use AI, and list on every reel which parts AI helped with (a script draft, subtitles, a thumbnail), or "none". ([AI transparency](../../03-charter.md#5-ai-transparency))
5. **Give quick feedback.** A short yes, no or fix on drafts within 3 days.
6. **Help us learn.** As our first creator, tell us what works and what doesn't so we can do this for other teachers too.

No contract, no exclusivity, no share of your income. You can stop at any time.

**One polite request, entirely optional:** if you ever grow big, please pay it forward by helping the next teacher, with time (mentoring, free workshops, collabs) or, if you like, a small share of what you earn above a comfortable level. It's never required, never checked, and changes nothing about our support. ([Details](../../03-charter.md#pay-it-forward-a-polite-request-never-a-requirement))

## The bigger picture

You'd be the first of many. Our goal is to become Pakistan's version of Complexly, the studio behind Crash Course and SciShow: many education creators, backed by hundreds of volunteers who pledge a few hours a week, with free tools for teachers and students. Your HLLM idea ("Humans logic lagao") fits this perfectly, and we'd love to build parts of it with you. See our [vision](../../01-vision.md).
