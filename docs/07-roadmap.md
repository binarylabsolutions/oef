# Roadmap

*Last updated: 30 Sep 2026*

We grow one stage at a time. A stage starts when the previous gate is met, not on a date; the months below are targets.

| Stage | Target timing | Creators | Contributors | Key outcomes | Tools |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Pilot | Months 0–3 | Rohail only | ~10 from our network | Rohail posts 4 reels a week from an 8-reel buffer; every video credited | ClickUp, Discord, Google Forms, Claude Project |
| 2. Small studio | Months 3–6 | 3 | ~30 | Levels and checklists in use; 2 volunteer leads; first teacher tool pilots | ClickUp templates per creator, Discord roles, shared Drive |
| 3. Open studio | Months 6–12 | 10 | 100+ | Auto-matching bot; public credits board; first monthly public report | Discord bot + ClickUp API, public credits page |
| 4. Studio at scale | Year 2+ | 10+ | 300+ | Flagship series; regional languages; free teacher and student tools; registered non-profit | Our own platform in this repo if needed |

## Gates

| Gate | To move from → to | Criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pilot → Small studio | 4 weeks with no missed posting slot; at least 70% of pledged hours delivered; Rohail wants to continue |
| 2 | Small studio → Open studio | All 3 creators still active after 2 months; a second ops lead in place; charter reviewed by a lawyer |
| 3 | Open studio → Scale | 100 active contributors for 2 months running; running costs funded for 12 months |

**Paid experts:** vetting and creator-funded hiring from Small studio; studio-funded experts from Open studio, only against donated budget already in the ledger ([09-paid-experts.md](09-paid-experts.md)).

**Money rule at every stage:** no donation is accepted until the public ledger is live ([08-public-ledger.md](08-public-ledger.md)).
