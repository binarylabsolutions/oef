## Why we exist

### 1. Millions of children aren't learning

- **25.1 million children aged 5–16 are out of school**: 35% of the age group. In Balochistan it is 69%; in Sindh, 44%. ([Pakistan Education Statistics 2023-24](https://pie.gov.pk/SiteImage/Publication/PES%202024.pdf))
- **80% of Pakistani children can't read and understand a simple text by the end of primary school.** ([World Bank, 2024](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090524113129017/pdf/P17920914d233b0701985a14a5d15f20e04.pdf))
- In rural Pakistan, **only 49% of grade 5 children can read a grade 2 story.** ([ASER 2023](https://stateofchildren.com/annual-status-of-education-pakistan-2023-provisional/))
- **Literacy is 63%**: 73% for men, 54% for women, and 44% for rural women. ([Economic Survey 2025-26](https://www.finance.gov.pk/survey/chapter_26/10_Education.pdf))

Being in school is not the same as learning. The crisis is in understanding, and that's where content can help.

### 2. People are already watching, just not learning

- **TikTok reaches 79.9 million Pakistanis, YouTube 54.3 million, Facebook 52.9 million.** ([DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan))
- The median Pakistani is **20.6 years old**. A young country spends hours a day on short videos, and very little of it teaches anything.
- Yet **139 million people are still offline**, and during COVID **TV reached about 95% of people against 36% for the internet**. ([World Bank/OECD case study](https://oecdedutoday.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Pakistan-Educational-TV.pdf)) Reaching everyone means phones, TV, radio and offline copies.

### 3. There is a gap nobody is filling

- Pakistan has strong curriculum lesson libraries (Sabaq Foundation, Taleemabad, Khan Academy, The Citizens Foundation's schools).
- What's missing is **Urdu curiosity content**: science that makes you wonder, documentaries about Pakistan's own land and history, creativity shows for children. Past generations grew up with PTV quiz shows and children's programmes; today's children have almost nothing like it in Urdu. ([research](research/pakistan-education-context.md))

### 4. Good teachers work alone

- Talented teachers already make brilliant education videos. Rohail Sarfraz, a science teacher of 15+ years, reached **487,000 views** on a single reel.
- But each works alone, with no editor, no fact-checker, no calendar, no team. Their reach depends on how many hours one person has.

### 5. Many people want to help, and have no way to

- Students, designers, editors, teachers and professionals would give a few hours a week to education, but there's no simple, credited, trustworthy way to do it.

### 6. AI changes what learning is for

- When any answer is one question away, memorising matters less, and **understanding, judgement and making** matter more.
- People who can't tell a good answer from a confident wrong one, whether it comes from a person, a rumour or an AI, are easy to mislead. ([10-learning-in-the-ai-era.md](10-learning-in-the-ai-era.md))

## What we want to achieve in 10 years (by 2036)

These are targets, set high on purpose. We measure ourselves against them every year.

| Goal | Target by 2036 |
| --- | --- |
| **A studio people know** | Flagship Urdu shows for curiosity, creativity and documentary, known by name across Pakistan, on phones, TV and radio |
| **Creators backed** | 100+ education creators supported, in Urdu and at least 4 regional languages |
| **People contributing** | 5,000+ contributors credited for their work, with a public record of every hour given |
| **Free content** | 1,000+ hours of free, fact-checked, openly licensed education content |
| **Teachers helped** | Free tools and lesson material used by 100,000+ teachers |
| **Beyond the internet** | Studio content reaching 1 million+ children outside school, through TV, radio and NGO partners |
| **Real learning** | Independent evidence that viewers learn, not just watch |
| **Trust** | Every piece free, credited, fact-checked, AI-disclosed; every mistake corrected in public; every rupee in the public ledger |
| **A model for others** | Our open platform, playbooks and assets used by education creators in other countries |
